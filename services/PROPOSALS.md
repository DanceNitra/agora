## The 2-minute tailor (do this for EVERY job)
1. First line = restate THEIR specific problem in your words (proves you read it). Replace [their thing].
2. Keep it SHORT (Upwork shows ~the first 2 lines — lead with value, not "Dear Hiring Manager").
3. Always offer the demo + a small first step. End with one question (invites a reply).
4. Swap the demo link to your real recording once you've made it.

## TEMPLATE A — support bot / customer-service AI job
> Hi — you want [an AI assistant that answers your customers from your site/FAQ]. The hard part isn't
> building a bot; it's building one that doesn't invent wrong answers and quietly damage trust.
>
> I build support agents grounded only in your real content — accurate answers, and a clean "I'm not
> sure, let me connect you" when it doesn't know, instead of making things up. I can show you a working
> demo of exactly this (a café support agent: it answers real questions and safely refuses ones outside
> its content) before you commit anything.
>
> Quick first step: point me at your site or FAQ and I'll wire a small demo on your own content this
> week, free to look at. What are the 3 questions your customers ask most?

>

> send a short plan today.
