Smile Clinic Slovakia (Bratislava) - Dental Clinic

ABOUT
Smile Clinic is an English-speaking dental clinic in central Bratislava (Tower 115, by the Danube, near the Slovak National Theatre), with a second clinic in Piestany. Lead dentist Dr. Marek Salka has 15+ years of experience and has performed over 1,000 implants, crowns and veneers since 2006.

SERVICES
Dental implants (Branemark and Ankylos brands), crowns (including Cerec same-day crowns), porcelain veneers, bridges, root canal treatment, surgical tooth extraction, dental hygiene and preventive care, and treatment under IV sedation.

PRICES (guide)
Veneer: 327 to 397 GBP. Crown: 327 GBP standard, or 397 GBP for a Cerec same-day crown. Root canal: 77 GBP per canal. IV sedation: 580 GBP. Consultations are free. X-rays are free. Credit card payments carry no extra charge (Visa, MasterCard and other major cards accepted).

GUARANTEE
Implants and cosmetic work are guaranteed; an implant that fails is replaced at no cost.
