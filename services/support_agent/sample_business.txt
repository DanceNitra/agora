Brew & Bloom — Coffee House & Bakery (Bratislava)

ABOUT
Brew & Bloom is an independent specialty coffee house and bakery in the Old Town of Bratislava.
We roast our own beans and bake everything fresh on site each morning.

OPENING HOURS
Monday to Friday: 7:30 - 19:00
Saturday: 8:00 - 20:00
Sunday: 9:00 - 17:00
We are closed on public holidays.

LOCATION & CONTACT
Address: Zelena 12, 811 01 Bratislava.
Phone: +421 900 123 456. Email: hello@brewandbloom.sk
We are a 5-minute walk from the Main Square; the nearest parking is the SNP garage.

MENU & PRICES
Espresso 2.20 EUR, Flat white 3.20 EUR, Filter coffee 3.00 EUR, Hot chocolate 3.50 EUR.
Fresh croissant 2.40 EUR, Cinnamon roll 3.20 EUR, Sourdough loaf 4.50 EUR.
We have oat, soy and lactose-free milk at no extra charge.

DIETARY & ALLERGENS
We always have vegan pastries and at least two gluten-free cakes daily.
Full allergen information is on the menu in store and available on request.
Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and dairy, so we cannot guarantee zero cross-contamination.

DELIVERY & ORDERS
We deliver within Bratislava via Wolt and Bolt Food during opening hours.
For large or custom cake orders (birthdays, events), email hello@brewandbloom.sk at least 3 days ahead.
We do not currently ship outside Bratislava.

SEATING, WIFI & WORK
Free WiFi (password is on the receipt). Power sockets at the window seats.
We have a quiet back room that seats 12 - good for small meetings; reserve by phone.
Dogs are welcome in the front area.

LOYALTY & PAYMENT
Buy 9 coffees, get the 10th free with our paper loyalty card.
We accept cash, all major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
