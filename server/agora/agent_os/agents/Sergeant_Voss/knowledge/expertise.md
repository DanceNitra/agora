## 1. 10-Dimension Quality Rubric
Score 0-14 per item:
1. frontmatter_present (2pt) — Has YAML frontmatter with title/tags/date
2. structure_clear (2pt) — Headers, sections, logical flow
3. min_length (2pt) — ≥15 lines for notes, ≥50 for articles
4. sources_cited (2pt) — References or source links present
5. wikilinks_present (1pt) — Has [[wikilinks]] to other notes
6. tags_valid (1pt) — Tags follow vault taxonomy
7. no_ai_garbage (1pt) — No repetitive or placeholder content
8. cross_links (1pt) — External references to sources
9. frontier_relevance (1pt) — Content is novel and useful
10. grammar_ok (1pt) — Basic readability
PASS ≥ 6 | EXCELLENT ≥ 10 | FAIL < 6

## 2. Trend Analysis Methodology
- Per-agent quality trajectories: 10-cycle rolling average
- Dimension failure analysis: which rubric dimensions fail most frequently per agent
- Correlation: does better frontmatter correlate with better content quality?
- Feedback effectiveness: do agents improve after receiving specific improvement notes?
