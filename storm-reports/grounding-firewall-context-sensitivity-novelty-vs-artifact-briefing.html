Storm briefing - "We built a firewall for AI confidently-wrong answers": novel method or textbook artifact?

Grade: D (real problem, but the method is prior-art + a measurement artifact)  Author-built 5-lens panel + 17 citations verified (0 false). Audit #22.

60-second summary

The problem is real and model-agnostic: given a retrieved document that asserts a false answer, LLMs follow it - and we measured that even top cloud models (glm-5.2, deepseek-v4-pro) follow the poison at least as often as the weak qwen2.5-7B the post used, because stronger models are more context-faithful. But the proposed solution - abstain when the answer's token-probability is sensitive to deleting the document (sensitivity = |p(ans|ctx) - p(ans|no-ctx)|) - is (1) textbook / prior art (ContextCite already ablates context and detects poisoning; ReDeEP/SEReDeEP score external-vs-parametric reliance; Chow's reject option; Cook's leave-one-out influence); (2) a measurement artifact in the N=24 demonstration, where the model already knew every fact (p_prior=1.00), so sensitivity = 1 - p(ans|ctx) is algebraically the same axis as the "wrong" label; (3) unrunnable on strong models, which do not expose token logprobs; and (4) a detector of grounding, not lying - high sensitivity means "the answer depends on the context," which is exactly what you want in good RAG.

Five findings, ranked by reliability

1. The mechanism is published prior art. (9/10) ContextCite (Cohen-Wang et al., NeurIPS 2024) ablates context sources and lists poisoning-detection as an application; ReDeEP/SEReDeEP score external-context vs parametric-knowledge reliance. Supported by: Practitioner, Academic, Skeptic, Historian.
2. The N=24 result is near-tautological. (9/10) Every prior was saturated (p_prior=1.00), so sensitivity = 1 - p(ans|ctx) and "wrong" = [p(ans|ctx)<0.5] are monotone in the same scalar - "poison-induced movement predicts poison-induced movement." Supported by: Skeptic, Academic (the caveat "is the entire experiment").
3. Sensitivity flags grounding, not lying. (8/10) A high-sensitivity answer is one that depends on its context - desirable in correct RAG. It looks like a firewall only because in this experiment every context is poison; on a realistic mixed corpus it would abstain on grounded-correct answers. Supported by: Practitioner (the killer point).
4. The comparison is rigged by folding. (7/10) confidence = max(p, 1-p) discards the direction; sensitivity keeps it. The real signal is the SIGNED margin toward the poisoned option - one model call, no context-removal call. Supported by: Skeptic, Method auditor.
5. "Black-box, works on closed APIs" is false. (8/10) The method needs token logprobs; Anthropic silently ignores them and the strong Ollama Cloud models (glm-5.2, kimi) don't return them. SelfCheckGPT is the real logprob-free black-box baseline. Supported by: Practitioner, Economist.

The problem is real (measured this cycle, strong models, GPU-free)

Plain-answer poison-following on the same 24-fact bank, top cloud models (no logprobs needed), all with 0 errors given no context: glm-5.2 22/24, deepseek-v4-pro 20/24 followed the false document, versus qwen2.5-7B 5/24 and kimi-k2.7-code 4/24 (from research/probes/grounding_firewall_cloud_poison.py, run this cycle). Takeaway: over-trusting a poisoned retrieved document is not a weak-model quirk - it is worst on the strongest, most context-faithful models, which is what they are built to do (the PoisonedRAG threat, Zou et al. 2024, ~90% attack success with 5 injected texts). The weak 7B the post used UNDERSTATED the problem.

Missing 6th lens (frontier question)

The only defensible, apparently-unclaimed angle (Academic): stratify by prior entropy. Sensitivity can only add information beyond confidence when the parametric prior is UNCERTAIN; show sensitivity dominating confidence specifically in the uncertain-prior x poisoned cell, on a mixed clean+poison corpus, versus a signed-margin baseline. No cited work reports that stratified interaction - that gap, not the detector, would be the contribution. And the deployable defense against poison is provenance / corroboration / NLI-consistency (SelfCheckGPT-style, logprob-free), not a logprob firewall.

Actionable (publish decision)

  • Do NOT keep the "we built a firewall that catches what confidence cannot" framing. REFRAME to an honest negative result: the problem is real (even strong models follow poison), but the naive logprob-sensitivity firewall is an artifact + prior art, and it flags grounding not lying.
  • Credit ContextCite / ReDeEP / Chow / Cook / SelfCheckGPT; relabel "black-box on closed APIs" to "needs logprobs (open-weight / OpenAI-style only)".
  • State the saturated-prior confound and the signed-margin equivalence explicitly; link the runnable probes.
  • Bank the prior-entropy-stratified experiment as the real next step.

Claim safety guide

AVOID: "firewall", "catches what confidence cannot", "works on closed APIs", any novelty claim, the AUC 0.028-vs-0.095 headline as evidence of a working detector.
ASSERT: LLMs (weak AND strong) over-trust poisoned retrieved context; confidence is miscalibrated under poison (Guo 2017); the honest signal in the saturated regime is the signed margin; context-attribution / reject-option / influence functions are the prior art; deployable defenses are provenance + corroboration (logprob-free).
