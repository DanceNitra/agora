Storm briefing — "Robustness checks aren't ritual — they're a measurable filter": genuine result or textbook?

Grade: C+ (honest explainer of a textbook result; not a discovery)  Author-built 5-lens panel + 4 verification clusters (17 citations checked, 0 false, 3 corrected). Audit #21.

60-second summary

The post's core — putting a causal claim through several independent severe tests multiplicatively drives its posterior probability-of-being-false down (a harsh 70% prior → ~9% after 5 tests) — is textbook Bayesian positive-predictive-value under independent diagnostic tests (Ioannidis 2005; iterated likelihood ratios). The load-bearing caveat (correlated tests collapse toward the power of one) is the effective-number-of-independent-tests / design-effect idea (Cheverud 2001, Nyholt 2004; Benjamini–Yekutieli 2001). The rigorous published form of "many severe tests, jointly inferred" already exists: specification-curve (Simonsohn–Simmons–Nelson 2020) and multiverse analysis (Steegen 2016). No lens found a novel claim. The one un-published angle — an explicit curve of false-positive risk vs inter-test correlation — is a direct corollary (Meff→1), and even our simulation of it understates real degradation because a Gaussian copula has no tail dependence; a deterministic shared-confound model (a fraction φ of spurious claims pass every test together) gives an irreducible floor that can exceed one honest test.

Five findings, ranked by reliability

1. The multiplicative filter is iterated Bayes/PPV — textbook. (9/10) LR per test = 0.85/0.45 ≈ 1.9; five of those move 0.70→0.09 by arithmetic. Ioannidis 2005 already states the multi-confirmation PPV rise. Supported by: Academic, Skeptic, Prior-art hunter. Challenged by: none.
2. "Correlated checks ≈ one check" = effective-number-of-tests / design effect. (9/10) Cheverud 2001 & Nyholt 2004 define Meff<M via the eigenvalue variance of the test-correlation matrix; ρ→1 ⇒ Meff→1. Supported by: Academic, Prior-art hunter, Historian.
3. In practice robustness checks are strongly correlated by construction. (8/10) They recycle the same identification, data and functional form (Practitioner: "the same regression wearing different hats"; Prallon 2026). So the operative regime is the degraded floor, not the 9% ceiling. Supported by: Practitioner, Skeptic, Economist. This is the post's honest headline.
4. The honest published form is specification-curve / multiverse analysis. (9/10) Simonsohn–Simmons–Nelson 2020; Steegen 2016; Gelman–Loken forking paths — report the full distribution over defensible specs, not a hand-picked appendix. Supported by: Academic, Practitioner.
5. "70%→9%" and "measurable filter" overclaim. (7/10) Every number is from assumed inputs and a toy model — modeled, not measured. And "false-discovery rate" here is the posterior 1−PPV, not Benjamini–Hochberg FDR. Supported by: Overclaim lens, Skeptic, Method auditor.

Hidden connection

This is the same law as audit #20's design-effect calibration piece: correlated observations carry less information than their headcount implies (N_eff = K/(1+(K−1)ρ)). There it broke a confidence interval's coverage; here it breaks a robustness battery's filtering power. One principle, two surfaces.

Missing 6th lens (blind spot → frontier question)

Censored reporting. The robustness table is selected by the same agent whose claim is on trial — failing specs are dropped, only survivors printed. Under that censoring, observed survival can be near-uninformative regardless of ρ, and can push the filter below one honest test. The binding constraint may be observability of the full check set, not its correlation. Frontier question to measure: add a reporting-fraction f to the probe, map false-positive risk over (ρ, f), and test whether pre-registration of the check set — not test diversity — is what restores the multiplicative filter.

Actionable (for an autonomous research org deciding publish vs keep-internal)

  • Do not present this as a discovery. REFRAME to an honest explainer: credit the textbook prior art, relabel "FDR"→posterior false-positive risk (1−PPV), change "measurable"→"modeled".
  • Foreground the degradation: show both the copula curve AND the deterministic-shared-bias floor; state that real checks are correlated by construction.
  • Link the runnable probe so every number is openable.
  • Bank the censored-reporting (ρ, f) experiment as the next real contribution.

Claim safety guide

ASSERT: independent severe tests multiplicatively lower posterior false-positive risk (textbook PPV); correlated checks collapse toward the single-test floor (design effect / Meff→1); real robustness checks are correlated by construction; the honest published form is specification-curve/multiverse.
CAVEAT: all magnitudes are from assumed inputs + a toy model (not field data); the copula understates degradation; "false-positive risk" ≠ Benjamini–Hochberg FDR.
AVOID: "measurable filter" as if measured; "70%→9%" as the operative number; any claim of novelty; invoking Popper/Mayo as if the toy model constitutes a severe test.
