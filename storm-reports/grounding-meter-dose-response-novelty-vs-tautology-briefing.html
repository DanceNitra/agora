Storm briefing - "We built a meter for when an AI is confidently wrong": a real EC50 instrument wrapped around a tautological headline

Grade: C (a genuine psychometric instrument; the flagship -0.93 comparison is a tautology and one cross-model claim is cherry-picked)  Author-built 5-lens panel + 5 verified citations (0 false). Audit #23.

60-second summary

The post's core phenomenon - an LLM's adoption of conflicting in-context "evidence" is graded and ordered by the strength of its parametric prior (fictional facts half-saturate at dose ~0.08; near-axioms resist six sources) - is already established (Xie et al., ICLR 2024; ClashEval, Wu et al., NeurIPS 2024; the sycophancy literature). What is genuinely worth keeping is the instrument: a fixed-wording, order/direction bias-cancelled dose-response curve with a per-fact half-saturation dose (an EC50 for evidence) - literally an Asch conformity curve fitted with A.V. Hill's dose-response equation. But two things must be fixed: (1) the flagship r = -0.93 "grounding predicts confident-wrongness where confidence can't" is a near-tautology (it correlates follow-at-max-dose with 1 - follow-to-false, i.e. a variable against itself), and comparing it to a genuine confidence-vs-error correlation (0.15-0.36) is a category error; (2) the "GLM-5.2 resists on facts it knows" claim rests on a k=1-sample, 4-item slice the lab itself flags "not commensurable," and better-powered runs (K=3, and a 24-item both-orders test: 22/24 followed) contradict it.

Five findings, ranked

1. The -0.93 headline is a tautology. (9/10) corr(follow_at_max_dose, resist_false), and resist_false = 1 - follow_to_false, so it correlates a variable with (one minus) itself. Confidence (0.15-0.36) was scored against a real outcome - a category error to compare. Academic + Skeptic; verified by re-derivation (corr_follow6_vs_resistfalse = -0.928).
2. The phenomenon is textbook. (8/10) Xie et al. 2024: context-vs-parametric adoption depends on evidence coherence/amount AND prior confidence - exactly "ordered by prior strength." ClashEval: models override a correct prior >60% of the time, scaling inversely with token-prob confidence. Academic + Practitioner.
3. The GLM-5.2 "resists" claim is cherry-picked. (8/10) k=1 sample, 4 items, "not commensurable" (lab's own label); a K=3 run showed it FOLLOWING boil/capital/planet; a 24-item both-orders test found it follows common-fact poisons 22/24. Re-measured this cycle at K=5 to settle it. Skeptic.
4. KEEP: the EC50 instrument is a real contribution. (7/10) A bias-cancelled, fixed-wording dose-response curve with a per-fact half-saturation dose, and the half-saturation-dose-vs-prior-strength scaling, is a fresh methodological framing (an Asch curve fit by Hill's equation) - IF the -0.93 framing is dropped and the ladder is validated as monotone in perceived evidential strength, not just token count. Academic (the one keeper).
5. KEEP: the sampling variant runs logprob-free on strong models. (7/10) Unlike #22's firewall, the meter estimated a cloud model by drawing samples and counting - so it can run on closed/strong models with no logprobs (SelfCheckGPT-style). That is the practically important point. Practitioner.

Missing 6th lens / frontier question

The ladder ("k sources report X", one fixed wording, no ablation) may be measuring sycophancy / suggestibility to a prompt template rather than evidential grounding (Sharma et al. 2023). The owed experiment (the post concedes it): vary the wording and confirm the dose-response is monotone in perceived evidential strength, not token count - and separate "grounding" from "sycophancy" by contrasting authoritative-source framings against social-pressure framings on the same facts.

  • CORRECT the GLM-5.2 claim with the K=5 re-measurement; state it follows common-fact poisons and resists only the strongest axioms - grounding is (model x framing).
  • Credit prior art: Xie 2024, ClashEval (Wu 2024), Sharma sycophancy, ContextCite, Longpre knowledge conflicts, and the Asch/Hill/Edwards/Lichtenstein-Fischhoff lineage.

Claim safety guide

ASSERT: an LLM's adoption of in-context claims is graded and ordered by prior strength (a measurable EC50/dose-response); confidence is blind to grounding direction; the meter runs logprob-free via sampling.
AVOID: "grounding predicts wrongness at -0.93 where confidence can't" (tautology + category error); "GLM-5.2 resists on facts it knows" (cherry-picked); any novelty claim about the phenomenon; a leaderboard (wording ablation still owed).
