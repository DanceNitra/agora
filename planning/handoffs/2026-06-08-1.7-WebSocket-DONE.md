### One wiring change vs the handoff (ordering-safe)

The handoff said to pass `event_bus=app.state.event_bus` into the `TrustEngine(...)` /
`TFTVerifier(...)` **constructors**. But those are created at `main.py:80`, and
`app.state.event_bus` doesn't exist until line ~122. Constructing with `event_bus` there
would always pass `None`. So — exactly as 1.1's `event_store` and 1.9's deps already do —
I added the optional `event_bus` constructor param (so the attribute exists / engines are
usable standalone) **and** inject the live bus by attribute right after `event_bus.start()`:

```python
app.state.trust.event_bus = app.state.event_bus
app.state.tft_verifier.event_bus = app.state.event_bus
```

This is the pattern that actually works given the init order, and keeps the constructors
backward-compatible.
