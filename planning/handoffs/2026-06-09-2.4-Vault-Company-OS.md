### What This Is

**Vault Company OS** — každý agent je zamestnanec vault firmy so svojou:
- **ROLE** — presná pozícia (Research Scout, Knowledge Curator, Idea Alchemist, Engineering Lead, Bridge Builder, Quality Assurance)
- **SOUL** — Big 5 personality, values, motivation, mood
- **SKILLS** — 14 vault-specific skills (research, writing, idea_generation, bridge_building, code_building, quality_audit, ...) s XP systémom
- **TOOLS** — scope nad RealActionEngine toolmi (write_note, write_article, run_script, etc.)
