=== Robustness: confidence-weighted voting (weight each vote by its |x|) ===
MEASURED: under confidence-weighting the amplification region is 53.8% of the plane (vs 53.3% for plain majority). Weighting the confident does NOT rescue it — confident reasoners are confident BECAUSE of the shared cue, so weighting leans HARDER on it.

VERDICT: LAW HOLDS — a real boundary exists. Aggregation helps only above a correlation-dependent competence threshold (c*~0.67 at high correlation); below it, a shared misleading cue makes the majority WORSE than one reasoner, and adding reasoners deepens the error. Pure Condorcet (always-helps) is the special case g>=0.5 or beta=0.
