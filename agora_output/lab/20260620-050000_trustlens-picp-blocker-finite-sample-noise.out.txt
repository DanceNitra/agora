=== Headline (for the gated draft) ===
A model truly at 90% coverage (calibration_error -0.05, NOT meant to be blocked) is FALSE-BLOCKED by the PICP blocker 17% of the time at n=30, 12% at n=50, down to 0.6% at n=200.
A weak-but-positive model (true R2=0.10) trips the negative-skill blocker 42% of the time at n=30, 25.1% at n=200.
Implication: gate the blockers on a minimum test-set size, or apply them to a one-sided confidence bound on PICP/R^2 rather than the point estimate, so a 'trust' score isn't itself noise-driven on small evals.
