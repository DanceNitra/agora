**Suggested copy / comment:**

A measured demonstration that a 95% Bayesian credible interval does NOT deliver 95% frequentist coverage when the model is misspecified - and that more data can make calibration worse, not better, by concentrating the posterior on the wrong value. Smallest model + the falsifier are in the post. Pushback welcome.
