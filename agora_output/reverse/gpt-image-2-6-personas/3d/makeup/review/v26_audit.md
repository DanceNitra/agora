## Three biggest remaining differences, with a fix direction

1. Tail and break, unchanged since v25. The brow still ends roughly where the reference tail begins, and the image-right brow has almost no tail. Fix: put the break at about 65 % of the head-to-tip length on both sides, then add a straight tail of about 0.4 to 0.5 ew toward the temple at about 20 degrees down. Taper the width linearly from about 0.25 ew at the break to about 0.03 ew at the tip. Keep the tip at or slightly above the bottom edge of the head. Use one template, mirrored.
2. Heads and overall thickness. Both heads are blunt, vertical blocks as thick as the body, and image-left lost the v25 dip into the nose. Fix: make the head narrower than the body (about 0.18 to 0.2 ew), cut the lower inner corner into a slanted edge that points down toward the nose, and drop the head opacity to about 30 to 40 % over the first 15 % of the length with near-vertical strokes. Reduce body thickness from about 0.3 to about 0.22 to 0.25 ew while keeping the approved average density.
3. Asymmetric texture and the marker fill on image-left. Image-right now shows strokes and tone variation; image-left is still solid black, and symmetry IoU fell to 0.853. Fix: generate both brows from the same stroke-based alpha (mirrored), with per-hair tone variation from dark brown to black and a slight specular term. Then re-check the IoU with the purple rim light switched off, so that lighting does not hide a texture difference.

Not judged: lashes (oversized, they reach toward the brow from below and make the brow read lower and heavier), skin tone, lighting, hair colour.
