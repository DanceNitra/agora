## Three biggest remaining differences, with a concrete fix

1. **The brow now floats high above the eye.** Thinning was done by raising the bottom edge (0.097 to 0.035 ew below the crest), so the whole brow moved up and the mid-brow gap to the lid is about 0.5 to 0.6 ew against about 0.3 ew in the reference. Fix: keep the 0.19 ew thickness but move the band down by about 0.05 ew, so top_minus_crest is about -0.11 ew and bottom_minus_crest about 0.08 ew. Lower the outer half (break to tail) more than the head, which also flattens the arch.
2. **The arch is still a rounded curve with no break.** Fix: make head-to-break one straight top-edge line at about 17 degrees, put a corner with a radius under 0.05 ew at 67 %, and from there run the tail straight down at about 20 degrees below horizontal. Lengthen the tail by about 0.15 ew so it reaches past the outer eye corner, ending in a point under 0.03 ew.
3. **The image-right head is a grey block and the texture is uniform ink hatching.** Fix: give the image-right head the same taper as image-left (0.12 ew at the inner end, slanted toward the nose, full width by 20 % of the length) and the same density as its body, so it stops reading grey. Across both brows, orient strokes upward at the head, diagonal in the body and sideways in the tail, and vary hair colour by about 10 to 15 % toward dark brown so the brow reads as hair, not ink.

Not judged: lashes (still oversized and reaching into the brow zone, which makes the brow look higher), skin, lighting, hair colour, approved brow colour and density.
