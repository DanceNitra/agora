## The one that was wrong, and how

Reference 2 went out on 22 August as "A 299, 107 (2001)". The 107 came from an author-supplied
journal_ref on the arXiv entry for cond-mat/0108472, which reads "Physica A 299/3-4, 107-120
(2001)". Crossref gives 461-474. The arXiv field is author-entered and was never corrected there;
the registered record is the one to cite. Corrected in the letter of 31 August.

## Why reference 4 was missed for weeks

Its registered title carries "Fractal", not "Sierpinski". A literature search keyed on the lattice
name returns nothing for the single most relevant paper on the exact model, published by the same
journal that later declined the manuscript. The Crossref title above confirms this from the
registration record rather than from our own search log.
