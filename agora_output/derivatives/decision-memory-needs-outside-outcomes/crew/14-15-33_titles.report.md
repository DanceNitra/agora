## Before the copy: three lines

- **Optimized for:** a number the listener can check against the probe, and the ranker-alibi image
  from the noir frame, so the fact survives the tone.
- **Rejected:** every line that says the ranker "failed" or "is useless", every line that says the
  outside credit "caught" or "detected" poisoning, and every line that states the project boundary
  helped.
- **Why:** the brief binds three truths. The ranking gain cannot be told from zero, the outside
  credit was the dataset label, and the project effect was not established. A title that breaks one
  of them is an overclaim that the article itself refutes.

### Recommendation

**#4: "AI Agent Memory: The Ranker Had an Alibi Worth 0.019".** It keeps "AI Agent Memory" at
character 0 for Spotify search, carries the episode's noir premise, and makes a claim the probe
prints. It does not say the ranker is useless, so it matches the article's "cannot tell it from
zero".

## 2. Article-side hook: the sentence a builder stops for

- **A (the main result):** On 160 engineering forks, random lessons from the same repository
  lifted Claude Opus 5.5 to 0.750, and our similarity ranker reached 0.769, a gap this run cannot
  tell from zero.
- **B (the self-grading result):** When Qwen was told how its past choices turned out, its lessons
  added 0.087, and when it judged its own choices, it did no better than having no lessons at all.
- **C (the poisoning result):** Flipping the outcome on about half of every recalled set dropped
  Opus from 0.825 to 0.637, and recalling only outside-credited lessons restored 0.825, where the
  credit was the dataset's own label.

Recommendation: **A** for the article and the episode description. It is the headline finding and
its caveat is inside the sentence. B is the strongest line for Reddit comments and X. C must never
run without its last clause.

## 3. Audience note (15)

- **Who:** engineers who build agents with a memory layer (LangGraph, CrewAI, custom RAG) and choose
  its retrieval: embedding model, top-k, reranker.
- **What they already believe:** better retrieval means better memory, so the next gain comes from
  a better ranker or embedding.
- **What they do not question:** whether the stored lesson carries a confirmed outcome, or who
  confirmed it.
- **The belief this episode breaks:** on these forks the value was in the outcome written into the
  lesson, and ranking added a margin this run cannot tell from zero.
- **Who it is NOT for:** listeners who want a leaderboard or a product pitch. The episode sells
  neither.

## 4. Reddit title for r/AI_Agents

Target: median 12 words, a number, first person doing the thing. Word counts were measured.

1. **I ranked my agent's past lessons by similarity. Random same-repo lessons came within 0.019**
   (14 words)
2. **I let an agent learn from its own choices: +0.087 told the outcome, nothing when self-judged**
   (16 words)
3. **I flipped the outcome on half my agent's recalled lessons. Accuracy fell from 0.825 to 0.637**
   (16 words)

Recommendation: **1.** It is the main finding, it is nearest the 12-word median, and it names the
thing every r/AI_Agents builder has tuned.

Notes for the poster:
- Title 2: "nothing" is the interval reading (-0.044, reaching 0.000). The body must give the numbers and
  say the self-judging arm is Qwen.
- Title 3: the body must say the outside credit was the dataset label, so it shows what the gate
  does with the signal and not that it detects an attack. "Half" matches the article's "about half
  of every recalled set".
- Never post the same title in two subreddits.
