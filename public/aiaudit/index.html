Fill in whatever parts of your system you have (pre-filled with a failing example). It runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

Answer the ones you can — skip the rest. The defaults are a deliberately broken example so you see a full report. Hover units for help.

1 · Did a change actually help?

Your A/B test — conversions out of users, per variant.

2 · Is your model training on its own output?

Model-collapse risk from synthetic / self-generated data.

3 · Do your agents copy each other?

Multi-agent / ensemble herding (skip if single-agent).

4 · Is your KPI / reward easy to game?

Reward-hacking / Goodhart — when a metric becomes a target.

5 · Are you controlling for the right things?

Causal/attribution — one variable you "control for". Adjusting for the wrong kind injects bias.
